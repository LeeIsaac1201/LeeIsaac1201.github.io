Who I am

I'm a final-year IT student, and an aspiring software engineer with a growing interest in fintech: products that have to earn trust while handling dense, high-stakes information. I've picked up a range of languages and tools along the way (Flutter/Dart, PHP, Java, C#, Scala), and that habit shapes how I think about design too: less about visual flourish, more about whether an interface holds up under real complexity. Working across both design and development on HomeHero and PawsBase has also pointed me toward roles where I'd get to sit with clients directly, translating what they need into something real, rather than staying heads-down as a software engineer all day. That's part of why I'm currently looking at consulting.

// disclosure

AI Use Statement

I used Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant) at several points while producing this portfolio. For HomeHero, I used AI to help generate and explore an initial feature set during ideation, which I then narrowed down myself based on realistic capstone scope. For PawsBase, I used Claude to help draft the case study write-ups (research synthesis, process descriptions, and the key decision analysis) based on real work I had already done, and to help generate the Flutter code for the redesigned health record screen, which I then reviewed, tested, and adjusted myself.

What stayed mine throughout: the actual design decisions (including the admin-role trade-off on HomeHero and the progressive-disclosure approach on PawsBase), the user interviews and their findings, the usability testing sessions and what came out of them, and the reasoning behind every trade-off discussed in these case studies. AI assistance sped up drafting and code scaffolding; the judgment calls and the work they're based on are my own.
