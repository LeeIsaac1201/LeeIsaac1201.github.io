Who I am

I'm a final-year IT student, and an aspiring software engineer with a growing interest in fintech: products that have to earn trust while handling dense, high-stakes information. I've picked up a range of languages and tools along the way (Flutter/Dart, PHP, Java, C#, Scala), and that habit shapes how I think about design too: less about visual flourish, more about whether an interface holds up under real complexity. Working across both design and development on HomeHero and PawsBase has also pointed me toward roles where I'd get to sit with clients directly, translating what they need into something real, rather than staying heads-down as a software engineer all day. That's part of why I'm currently looking at consulting.

AI Use Statement

This portfolio represents my own work, including the ideation and refinement of HomeHero’s feature set, the drafting and analysis for PawsBase case studies, and the creation and testing of the redesigned Flutter health record screen. The design decisions (such as the admin role trade-off on HomeHero and the progressive disclosure approach on PawsBase), the user interviews and findings, the usability testing sessions, and the reasoning behind every trade-off are entirely mine, reflecting the judgment calls and real work I carried out throughout these projects. Artificial intelligence (Claude) was used only in producing the portfolio itself, for example to support idea generation and refine the video presentation script, while all project outcomes and decisions remain my own.
